Refund Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This policy explains how to cancel a DuoVox subscription and how refunds work. How you do either depends on where you bought: through our online checkout, or in the Microsoft Store. It forms part of our Terms of Use (section 10a).

1. Purchases through our online checkout

Our online checkout is provided by Paddle.com, our online reseller. Paddle.com is the Merchant of Record for all orders placed through our online checkout, and refunds for those orders follow Paddle's refund policy. In short:

  • Asking for a refund: you can request a refund within 14 days of a purchase at paddle.net (choose "Request refund"), or through the link in the receipt email Paddle sent you. Paddle reviews each request under its refund policy.
  • Your legal rights: consumers may also have a legal right to cancel a purchase within a set period under the law of their country. Nothing in this policy takes those rights away.
  • Where the money goes: an approved refund is paid back to the payment method you used.
  • What a refund ends: when a purchase is refunded in full, the plan or the Pay-As-You-Go credit it bought ends at once.

Cancelling a subscription: you can cancel at any time using the link in your receipt email from Paddle, at paddle.net, or by asking us at info@duovox.net. You keep your paid features until the end of the period you have paid for, and you are not charged again. Cancelling stops future payments; it does not by itself refund the current period.

Purchases through our online checkout are made with David Arthur Software Ltd (company number 14318326), registered office 1 Sherbrooke Close, Kirkbymoorside, York, YO62 6LJ, United Kingdom, with Paddle.com as reseller.

2. Purchases in the Microsoft Store

Plans and Pay-As-You-Go credit bought in the Microsoft Store are sold and billed by Microsoft, and refunds for them are handled by Microsoft under its own refund policy. You can ask for a refund from your order history in your Microsoft account (account.microsoft.com/billing/orders), and cancel a subscription under Services & subscriptions in the same account. When you cancel, you keep your paid features until the end of the period you have paid for.

3. Questions

If something has gone wrong with a purchase, or you believe you are entitled to a refund for unused subscription time, contact us at info@duovox.net and we will review your request on a case-by-case basis. Please quote the device ID shown in the App under About → Contact, and, for an online checkout purchase, the order number from your receipt.
