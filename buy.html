How it works

  1. 1. Choose and payPick a plan or hours below and pay by card or PayPal.
  2. 2. Install DuoVoxGet DuoVox free from the Microsoft Store.
  3. 3. ActivateClick Activate in DuoVox after paying, or enter your code in the app under Plans & hours. How?

After you pay by card or PayPal on duovox.net, the confirmation page shows a code and an Activate in DuoVox button. Install DuoVox from the Microsoft Store, then click Activate in DuoVox. Or open Plans & hours in the app, choose Have a DuoVox code?, enter the code and press Use code. Hours are added to that computer. A plan becomes active on that computer, which then shows a code for adding your other computers (up to three). Keep your code; if you lose it, email info@duovox.net with your Paddle receipt and we will find it.
