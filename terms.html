2a. Usage Limits and Continuation

Standard and Professional tiers include a set number of AI usage hours per billing cycle. Usage is measured as wall-clock time while AI transcription sessions are active — paused, idle, and silence periods do not count. The App will notify you at 80% and at 100% of your monthly hours. At 100% you may choose to continue at your subscribed quality level using Pay-As-You-Go credits, continue at the Free offline quality level (lower accuracy but no internet required) for the remainder of the session, or end the session and wait for your next billing cycle. The App will never automatically charge you beyond your subscription without your explicit confirmation. Unused subscription hours do not carry over to the next billing cycle; purchased Pay-As-You-Go credits do not expire.
