2. Plans and Pay-As-You-Go

The App offers the following plans. **The price for your country is shown in the Microsoft Store before you buy, in your own currency, and that is the price you pay.** Current prices are also published at duovox.net. We describe here what each plan includes rather than repeating prices, so that this document cannot fall out of step with what the store actually charges.

  • Free: On-device transcription using local speech recognition. Works entirely offline with no internet connection required. In-app promotional messages or light banner advertising may be displayed.
  • Standard (monthly or annual): Live AI-powered transcription and translation across 33 supported languages (Professional covers 43; translation is available for more languages than can be transcribed — the current per-plan list is published at duovox.net/languages). Includes 15 hours of AI usage per month and document translation. No advertising. Additional usage beyond the included hours is available via Pay-As-You-Go.
  • Professional (monthly or six-monthly): Enhanced terminology recognition for specialist vocabulary, advanced AI translation, and 40 hours of AI usage per month. Includes subtitle export (SRT and VTT), file transcription and translation (audio and video), Translation Glossary, and Private Mode.
  • Pay-As-You-Go: No subscription required. Buy credit at an hourly rate in either Standard or Professional quality, in packs of 1, 10 and 25 hours; the larger packs cost less per hour. Credits never expire. Available both as a stand-alone option (no subscription) and as a top-up for subscribers who exceed their monthly hours.

How credits and included hours are used together depends on the quality of the session. If you have no active subscription, all usage is drawn from your credits. If you do have a subscription and the session runs at the quality your subscription includes, your included hours are used first and your credit balance is then drawn on automatically once they run out, with nothing for you to do. If a Professional credit balance lets you run at a higher quality than your subscription includes — for example a Standard subscriber using Professional hours — that session is funded entirely from your credit balance, and your included hours are not consumed until the credits are exhausted. AI features stop only once both your included hours and your credits are exhausted.

All paid plans are billed through the app store from which you bought the App, and are subject to that store's terms. The Standard annual plan costs less than paying monthly for twelve months, and the Professional six-month plan costs less than paying monthly for six months. The exact saving depends on the prices in your own currency, which the store shows you before you buy.

2a. Usage limits and continuation

Standard and Professional include a set number of AI usage hours per billing cycle. Your allowance is granted monthly, and it is granted monthly for every plan length — an annual or six-month plan gives you the same hours each month, not one month's hours for the whole term. Usage is measured as the time an AI transcription session is running, from the moment you start it until you stop it; time outside a running session is never counted.

The App will notify you at 80% and at 100% of your monthly hours. At 100% you may choose to continue at your subscribed quality level using Pay-As-You-Go credits, continue at the Free offline quality level (lower accuracy but no internet required) for the remainder of the session, or end the session and wait for your next billing cycle. The App will never automatically charge you beyond your subscription without your explicit confirmation.

Unused subscription hours do not carry over to the next billing cycle; purchased Pay-As-You-Go credits do not expire. If your included hours and your credits both run out during a live session, AI transcription and translation stop at that point; the App continues to work in Free offline mode.

3. Internet and cloud services

Paid features require an active internet connection and access to cloud-based AI services. AI transcription and translation are provided through third-party service providers. Service availability depends on both DuoVox infrastructure and third-party provider uptime.

When a session is served by AI — on a subscription, or on any session funded by Pay-As-You-Go credits — the audio of that session and the text produced from it leave your device and are processed by those providers in order to return a result. They are processed in real time and are not stored by us.

When a session runs in Free offline mode, transcription and translation run entirely on your machine and no audio, transcript or translation is sent anywhere. Note that "Free" describes the plan you are on, not necessarily how a given session runs: if you hold Pay-As-You-Go credits without a subscription, sessions funded by those credits are AI sessions and are processed as described above until your credits are exhausted.

Separately, and on every tier including Free, the App sends us a short summary at the end of each session: the app version, your tier, the session length, which speech engine served it and whether it fell back to a backup engine, counts of errors and of captions produced, suppressed or corrected, and summary measurements of your microphone signal quality such as signal-to-noise, level and clipping. These summaries never contain audio, transcripts or translations. They are sent with a randomly generated device identifier created and stored on your device; it is not linked to your name, but it does let us associate reports from the same installation. The App also sends crash reports containing the error type, the first line of the technical stack trace and the app version, and a note when a previous run did not shut down cleanly. All of this is described in full in the Privacy Policy.
