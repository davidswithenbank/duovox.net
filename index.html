How It Works

1

Choose Your Languages

Select a primary language and a target language. DuoVox offers 62 languages — 57 on Professional, 53 on Standard, and 31 that work offline on Free. See exactly what each plan covers.

2

Start Talking

Press Start. DuoVox listens to what your PC is playing, such as a call or a video, or to the people in the room through your microphone, and starts writing it down straight away.

3

See Both Languages

Each language gets its own panel. You see your words on the left and the translation on the right — in real time.

4

Keep the Conversation

DuoVox detects which language is being spoken and puts each sentence in the right panel. Just talk naturally — it keeps up.

Accuracy Disclaimer

DuoVox uses AI-powered speech recognition and machine translation to assist communication. Like all transcription and translation tools, results may contain errors and should not be treated as a verbatim or certified record. DuoVox is an assistive communication aid and does not replace a qualified human interpreter for regulated or high-stakes contexts. Accuracy can be affected by background noise, accents, microphone quality, overlapping speech and domain-specific terminology. DuoVox is a communication aid and should not be relied upon as the only source of meaning in legal, medical, safety-critical, financial or official situations. Always verify important information independently.
