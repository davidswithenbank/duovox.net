Both sides of a conversation need to be supported by the plan you choose. If either language is unavailable on a plan, that speaker will not be captioned.

Free runs entirely on your device and never sends audio anywhere. Standard and Professional use online services for higher accuracy, and fall back to on-device processing if the connection drops.

Supported does not mean identical accuracy. Accuracy varies by language, accent, microphone and background noise, and some languages perform noticeably better than others on the same plan. Treat this page as a list of what is available, not a guarantee of how well it will perform for you.

Language availability depends on third-party services and on-device models and may change without notice. We update this page when it does; the date below shows when it was last generated.

DuoVox is an assistive aid, not a substitute for a qualified human interpreter, and is not certified for regulated medical or legal use. See our Terms of Use.

Regional variants (for example Traditional Chinese and British English) are selected separately in the app and are not listed individually here.
