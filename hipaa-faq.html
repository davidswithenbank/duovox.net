DuoVox, privacy & data handling

DuoVox is an assistive tool that produces automated, real-time speech-to-text and translation. It is a software aid only. It is not a certified, verified, or human interpretation or translation service, and it does not replace a qualified human interpreter or translator.

DuoVox is not a medical device, and it is not HIPAA-certified, regulatory-approved, or certified for clinical, diagnostic, or treatment use. Its output is not a certified, sworn, or official translation or record.

Automated transcription and translation can be wrong. You must not rely on DuoVox as the sole or authoritative source of meaning, or as the authoritative record, in any medical, legal, safety-critical, financial, immigration, law-enforcement, or other high-stakes situation. Always verify anything important by other means.

For details of how your data is handled, including our processing modes and retention practices, please see our Privacy Policy.
